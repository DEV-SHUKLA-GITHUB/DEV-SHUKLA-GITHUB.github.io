Channel Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Channel Publisher is a private desktop tool used by one person to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), it uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and set their details (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists, publish times, comments) on the owner's own channels, and to read those channels' analytics and reports.

Where data is kept

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored in the computer's encrypted system keychain. No data is sold, shared, or sent to any server run by this tool. Video text and sampled frames may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft titles and descriptions, under Anthropic's terms.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Removing access, or deleting the tool's local data folder, deletes everything it stored.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
