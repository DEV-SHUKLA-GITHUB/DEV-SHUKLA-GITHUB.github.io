Channel Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Channel Publisher is a private desktop tool used by one person to manage their own YouTube channels and to share those videos on their own Facebook Page, Instagram account and Threads profile. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), it uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and set their details (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists, publish times, comments) on the owner's own channels, and to read those channels' analytics and reports.

Where data is kept

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored in the computer's encrypted system keychain. No data is sold, shared, or sent to any server run by this tool. Video text and sampled frames may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft titles and descriptions, under Anthropic's terms.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Removing access, or deleting the tool's local data folder, deletes everything it stored.

Facebook, Instagram and Threads

With the owner's permission (Meta login), the tool uses the Meta Graph API, Instagram API and Threads API to publish the owner's own videos and posts (Reels, Page posts, Threads posts with a link) on the owner's own Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and Threads profile, and to read basic details of those accounts (name, follower count, publishing quota) and of the posts it published. It does not read, store or process data about any other person. Meta access tokens are stored only in the computer's encrypted system keychain. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

The tool stores no data about anyone other than its owner, and keeps nothing on any server. To delete everything it holds:

  1. Remove its access: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites → remove the app; Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → remove; Google → myaccount.google.com/connections → remove.
  2. Delete the tool's local data folder and its keychain entries on the owner's computer.

Removing access stops all further publishing immediately. Posts already published stay on Facebook, Instagram or Threads until the owner deletes them there.

Contact

Use the contact email shown on the Google or Meta sign-in screen for this app.
