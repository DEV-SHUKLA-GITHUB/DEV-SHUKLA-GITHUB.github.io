Channel Publisher

Channel Publisher is a private, single-user desktop tool. Its owner uses it on their own computer to publish videos to their own YouTube channels: uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists, scheduling publish times, and reading the channels' own analytics.

It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, its user agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service.
